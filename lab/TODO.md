# Interactive Graphics Lab Topics

1. **Vectors and Coordinate Systems** — [Built](./vectors-and-coordinate-systems/vectors-and-coordinate-systems.md.html)
   - Build one vector from three basis directions.
   - Visualize vector addition, the dot product, angles, and projection.

2. **Transforms and Matrix Composition**
   - Adjust translation, rotation, and scale.
   - Compare different matrix orders and parent-child transforms.

3. **Building the View Matrix**
   - Move a camera and its target.
   - Visualize the camera basis, world-to-view transformation, and resulting camera-space scene.

4. **Perspective Projection and the View Frustum**
   - Build on the camera-projection pilot.
   - Explore field of view, aspect ratio, near and far planes, clipping, and the projected image.

5. **From Triangles to Pixels**
   - Move a triangle across a pixel grid.
   - Inspect edge functions, coverage rules, winding, and backface culling.

6. **Barycentric Coordinates and Vertex Interpolation**
   - Drag triangle vertices and sample points.
   - Visualize barycentric weights and interpolated colors, normals, and UVs.

7. **Depth Testing and the Depth Buffer**
   - Reorder overlapping geometry and inspect per-pixel depth values.
   - Demonstrate depth precision loss and z-fighting.

8. **Perspective-Correct Interpolation**
   - Compare affine and perspective-correct UV interpolation on a tilted quad.
   - Make the resulting texture distortion immediately visible.

9. **Normals and Basic Lighting**
   - Manipulate lights, surfaces, and normals.
   - Break down ambient, diffuse, and specular contributions.

10. **Texture Sampling and Mipmaps**
    - Zoom and tilt a textured surface.
    - Compare nearest, bilinear, trilinear, and anisotropic filtering alongside mip levels.
